Hi, I'm Anjali

Over the years I've worked a few different jobs. Writing is the one thing that stayed.

I taught English Literature to high schoolers for a year. Before that, I was just a student who read too much and talked too little about it. Somewhere in between, writing stopped being a class subject and became the thing I actually do — late at night, in the margins of everything else.

Think of this site as a gallery of my brain — a few different rooms, all built from the same haunt for words. Pick the one you're here for.
